# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 6, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Josiah Vranyes ("we," "our," "us") collects, uses,
and protects personal information in connection with the Hermes SMS Assistant ("the
Service"), a personal AI assistant you can reach by text message.

Please read this policy carefully.
By using the Service, you agree to the practices described below.

## The Service

The Service is a personal AI assistant that you can text at **[phone number]**.
When you send a message to the assistant, it automatically replies to provide conversational
assistance, such as:

- Answering questions
- Providing directions
- Helping manage email and calendar
- General personal assistance

Each message you send receives a reply from the assistant.
The assistant responds using automated AI technology.

## Information We Collect

When you use the Service, we collect:

- **Your phone number** — the number you text from.
- **Message content** — the text of the messages you send to, and receive from,
  the assistant.
- **Recent message history** — a short record of our most recent messages, kept
  only in memory so the assistant can follow the conversation.
- **Information you ask the assistant to remember** — facts and preferences you
  choose to share with the assistant.
- **Message metadata** — such as timestamps and delivery status, needed to operate
  the Service.

We do not collect this information from any other source, and you are not required
to provide anything other than the message you choose to send.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

- Respond to the text messages you send to the assistant.
- Maintain conversation context so the assistant can understand and follow along.
- Perform the tasks you ask the assistant to do.
- Operate, maintain, and protect the Service.
- Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

## Automated Processing

The Service uses automated AI technology to generate replies to your messages.
To provide the Service, your messages may be transmitted to and processed by:

- **SMS providers** — such as Telnyx, which delivers text messages to and from your
  phone.
- **AI service providers** — companies that operate the AI models used to generate
  the assistant's replies.
- **Other service providers** — vendors that assist us in providing and delivering
  the Service.

These providers help us deliver the Service to you.

## Mobile Information Sharing

Your mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties for promotional
or marketing purposes.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent;
this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We will not share your opt-in to an SMS campaign with any third party for purposes
unrelated to providing you with the services of that campaign.
We may share your Personal Data, including your SMS opt-in or consent status, with
third parties that help us provide our messaging services, including but not limited
to platform providers, phone companies, and any other vendors who assist us in the
delivery of text messages.

## Consent, Opt-Out, and Help

By texting the assistant, you consent to receive text message replies from the Service.
Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Message frequency varies — the assistant replies to each message you send.

Message and Data Rates may apply.

To opt out at any time, **reply STOP to opt out**.

To get help, **reply HELP for help**.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

## Data Retention

Short-term message history is kept only in memory and is used solely to continue
your current conversation.
Information you ask the assistant to remember is retained to provide the Service,
and you may ask the assistant to forget it at any time.
You may also contact us to request deletion of information about you, as described
below.

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect against unauthorized
access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.
However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot
guarantee absolute security.

## Your Rights

You may ask us, at any time, to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate information.
- Delete the information we hold about you.
- Stop sending you messages (by replying STOP, as described above).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.
We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe.

If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under the California
Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), including
the right to know, delete, and correct your personal information, and the right
not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.
